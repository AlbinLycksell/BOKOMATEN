## Quick orientation for this design system

- **Brand name**: Switchboard. Verkstad is the design language.
- **Colors**: havsblå navy, linne off-white, signaloranje (sacred — only CTA / akutnotis / wordmark dot), tallgrön positive, larmröd critical, warm grays. Defined in `colors_and_type.css`.
- **Type**: Geist (Söhne substitute — flag this when shipping). Sentence case, tabular numerals, two weights only in product UI.
- **Pill buttons** are signature.
- **No emoji, no gradients, no glassmorphism, no illustrations, no stock photography.**
- **Voice**: du never ni; concrete over abstract; Magnus's voice not the corporation's.
- The four-question test: every visual decision must affirmatively answer at least one of — does this make Magnus trust us, save Lena 12 minutes, give Alex one fewer mistake when tired, or help Inger get answered.
